# RateMyGuin privacy policy

_Last updated 6 November 2026_

RateMyGuin is an independent app for finding, rating and ranking pints of Guinness. It is not affiliated with Guinness
or Diageo. This policy says what the app collects and why. If the app changes what it stores, this page changes too.

## What RateMyGuin stores

- **Your email address**, to sign you in with a one-time code. It is never shown to other people.
- **Your profile**: a handle, a display name, and optionally your home city. You can make your profile private.
- **Your ratings**: scores, an optional note and price, the time, and the pub. You can post a rating anonymously,
  and then your name isn't shown with it.
- **A location check when you rate.** When you rate a pint, the app checks your phone's position, and how accurate it
  is, against the pub, so the rating counts as a verified visit. That position is stored with the rating to stop fake
  reviews. It is **never shown to anyone else**, and the app does not track your location at any other time.
- **Photos**: a photo of your pint stays on your phone for now. It isn't uploaded.

## What other people see

Your handle and display name (unless your profile is private), and your ratings without their location (and without
your name, if you rated anonymously). Pub staff can see and reply to ratings of their pub, the same way.

## What RateMyGuin does not do

- No advertising, no ad tracking, no selling or sharing of your data.
- Your data is never combined with data from other companies.

## Where it is kept

Accounts and ratings are stored by Supabase, in London. Sign-in emails are sent by Resend. Pub names and locations
come from OpenStreetMap (© OpenStreetMap contributors).

## Deleting your data

Email [dan124@live.ie](mailto:dan124@live.ie) from the address you sign in with, and your account, profile and
ratings are deleted.

## Children

RateMyGuin is about alcohol and is only for people of legal drinking age (18 in the UK and Ireland).
